Must-Visit Places

Kashi Vishwanath Temple

One of the most famous Hindu temples dedicated to Lord Shiva. A cornerstone of faith in Varanasi.

Sarnath

A short drive from Varanasi, this is the deer park where Lord Buddha gave his first sermon. A peaceful and historic site.

A vast, green campus housing the New Vishwanath Temple and the Bharat Kala Bhavan museum.

Famous Ghats

Dashashwamedh Ghat

Famous for: The spectacular evening Ganga Aarti ceremony. It's the busiest and most vibrant ghat.

View on Map

Assi Ghat

Famous for: Morning yoga, a more relaxed atmosphere, and the confluence of the Ganga and Assi rivers.

View on Map

Manikarnika Ghat

Famous for: The main cremation ghat. A powerful place to observe Hindu funeral rituals. Photography is strictly prohibited.

View on Map

Local Delicacies

Kachori Sabzi

The classic Banarasi breakfast. Spicy, fried kachoris served with a flavorful potato curry.

Try at: Ram Bhandar

Tamatar Chaat

A unique chaat made with tomatoes, boiled potatoes, spices, and sev. A tangy and savory delight.

Try at: Kashi Chat Bhandar

Lassi

Thick, creamy yogurt-based drink served in a 'kulhad' (earthen cup), often topped with malai or fruit.

Try at: Blue Lassi Shop

Malaiyo

A seasonal (winter-only) dessert made from milk froth, saffron, and sugar. It's incredibly light and melts in your mouth.

Try at: Shreeji Sweets

Area Guide

Near the Main Ghats

Best for: Atmosphere, walking to temples, and boat rides.

Keep in mind: Can be noisy, lanes are very narrow, and no car access to most hotels.

Assi Ghat Area

Best for: A more relaxed, quieter vibe with many cafes. Popular with long-stay tourists.

Keep in mind: Further from the Kashi Vishwanath temple (30-min walk).

Cantonment (Cantt.) Area

Best for: Modern hotels, easy car/bus access, and proximity to the railway station.

Keep in mind: Far from the old city and ghats. You will need to take a rickshaw (20-30 mins) for all sightseeing.

Local Transport

Auto-Rickshaws & E-Rickshaws

Most common way to get around. E-Rickshaws are best for the main ghat areas.

Important: Always agree on a fare *before* you start your trip.

Info & Help

Ganga Aarti Info

Ganga Aarti Ceremony

  • Location: Dashashwamedh Ghat.
  • Time: Around 6:30 PM (after sunset).
  • Tip: Arrive by 5:30 PM to get a good spot.

Cultural Do's & Don'ts

Key Tips

  • DO dress modestly, covering shoulders and knees.
  • DO remove your shoes before entering temples.
  • DON'T take photos of cremations at Manikarnika Ghat.
  • DON'T point the soles of your feet at people or idols.

Developer's Note: An OS Concept

This app's tabbing system is inspired by a core Operating System concept called Context Switching. Just like an OS switches between different programs (processes), this app 'switches context' by hiding the old tab and showing the new one, ensuring only one 'process' (the active tab) uses the screen at a time.
